Female reproductive reference organs

NIH 3D Human Reference Atlas, Visible Human Female · U.S. National Library of Medicine. This is a separate reference set, not the same body as the Z-Anatomy whole-body atlas.

Available: Uterus and cervix; Left and right ovaries; Left and right uterine tubes; Urinary bladder; Sacrum, coccyx and pelvic bones.

Not included: Vagina; Complete urethra; External genitalia; Pelvic floor muscles; Whole-body skin, muscles, head and limbs. Absence from this set is not anatomical absence.

What Forma changed

Original female coordinates retained; one uniform 10× scene-unit conversion and translation shared by all files; transforms baked; display material; Draco compression. No deformation, hole filling or male geometry. The shared female reference positions and proportions are retained. No male-derived anatomy is included in this scene.

Mesh and content checks

40 original meshes and 4 study groups. 28 meshes have open boundary edges; no non-manifold edges were detected. 8180/8180 sampled surface rays selected successfully. Empty holes can still allow a ray through; these research surfaces are not claimed watertight.

Beginner Find: 19 eligible; Advanced Find: 42; Name that structure: 20 at aspect 1. Gates vary with viewport. Browser outline/orbit verification remains pending because the supported preview is unavailable.

Teaching gaps

  • Tubal opening patches (NIH source annotation under review): location, function, clinical. Source labels these abdominal ostia, but the supplied coordinates are at the uterine horns. Excluded from quizzes pending anatomical review.

Previous v12 subset

The retired Anatria3D intermediate subset had 264 female HRA meshes and 11 study groups. The import path did not deform male anatomy, but did not check every mesh against an individual NIH page. Its full inventory, source files and importer are retained in the source download's docs/female-audit directory. It is no longer the active reference view.
