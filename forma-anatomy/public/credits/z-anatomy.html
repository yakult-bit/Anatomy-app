What changed in Forma

The original FBX geometry was converted to GLB, its original transforms were baked, and its scale was uniformly normalized. Clinical display colors were assigned. Non-anatomical atlas annotations and origin/insertion markings were excluded. Anatomical joints, ligaments, fascia, and covering membranes are retained. Redundant triangles were reduced within a small error tolerance and the assets were Draco-compressed. Core mesh groups were mapped to Forma’s existing nursing content. Individual components retain their source names and can be inspected separately. The flat study board uniformly resizes original geometry for each preview; inspection translates original geometry without deforming it. Body view preserves anatomical sizes and positions.

Model coverage

The imported source contains male anatomy. It does not provide a female model or a separate subcutaneous fat layer. Identification and recall quizzes cover all imported structures and groups. Nursing notes and function matching cover the core study groups; individual pieces link back to their group notes where available. Clinical markers are approximate learning aids, not clinical placement guides.
